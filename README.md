# WIConnect

The Android companion to [WIland](https://github.com/ne-wtn/WIland-Releases), the dynamic island for Windows. Control your PC from your phone: a pad of shortcuts for the app you're in, a pointer and keyboard, your windows, timers, media, the clipboard and the Shelf.

## Install

1. On your Android phone, open the [latest release](https://github.com/ne-wtn/WIConnect-Releases/releases/latest) and download the `.apk`.
2. Open it. Android asks once to allow installing apps from your browser; allow it, then install.
3. In WIland on your PC, open **Settings → WIConnect**, show the pairing code, and scan it with WIConnect.

Your phone and PC need to be on the same Wi-Fi. WIConnect needs Android 10 or newer.

## Updates

WIConnect checks here when you open it and shows **WIConnect x.y.z is out** on its home screen. Tap it to update.
